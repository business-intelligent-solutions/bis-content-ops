# Opinions & Hot Takes — vShopper

> One strong opinion per blog post, max. These are the "things a lot of people think but don't say" Shany asked for — grounded in the actual market, not invented for shock value.

## On contracts vs. SIM-only

- **If your phone still works, a new contract is usually the wrong move financially — and almost nobody in a kiosk will tell you that.** SIM-only exists specifically for people who don't need a new handset. Steering someone into a 24/36-month contract they don't need is a short-term win and a long-term reason they don't come back.
- **"Free upgrade" isn't free — it's financed into the monthly cost.** Worth saying plainly instead of letting the word "free" do the marketing.

## On buying a phone

- **The newest flagship is rarely the right phone for most people.** Last year's model at a lower price does 90% of what most people actually use a phone for. A post that only pushes the newest release every time reads as an ad, not advice.
- **Pre-owned/refurbished phones are a legitimately good option, not a fallback for people who "can't afford new."** Worth normalising rather than treating as the budget-shame option.

## On the industry

- **Franchise retailers living and dying by store count is a scale story, not a service story.** AliSom competing on 60+ stores and awards is true and also not the same thing as a customer's actual phone getting fixed quickly. Worth a passing, non-disparaging contrast when relevant — never a direct attack on a named competitor.
- **Data pricing in South Africa is a legitimate, relatable gripe** — safe, evergreen territory for a hot take that resonates without being controversial.

## How to use these

- One per post, backed by something specific from `stats.md` where possible.
- Keep any competitor mention factual and light — a contrast, not a takedown.
