# Voice — vShopper

> Read this before writing any vShopper blog post.
> Source: live site copy (vshopper.co.za, pulled 03/09/2026) + Shany's direction to punch up the personality — the current site is functional and plain; the blog is where the brand gets to have a voice.

## Who is writing

vShopper is a Vodacom franchise mobile retailer — new and pre-owned phones (Apple, Samsung, Huawei, Honor), SIM-only and contract plans, business connectivity, POS. B2C, ecommerce-first, 20+ stores across South Africa (Vodacom, Vodacom Chatz, Vodacom 4U). Run by Surtie Mobile (Pty) Ltd out of Maitland, Cape Town.

The site today: friendly, accessible, solution-oriented, almost no personality ("Buy it. Love it. Get rewarded," "our team compares the options so you don't have to"). The blog is the place to add what the site doesn't have yet: wit, dad jokes, relatable hot takes, the kind of writing someone forwards to a group chat instead of skimming past.

## The brief, in one line

**Witty, funny, relatable — the friend who actually knows phones and isn't trying to upsell you.** Puns and dad jokes are welcome. So are jokes about society/culture at large (data prices, load shedding, group chat behaviour, the group chat person who still has a cracked screen from 2019) as long as they land and don't punch down at the reader.

## Sentence rhythm

- Short sentences. Mostly under 20 words.
- Start with the answer or the joke, then the substance.
- One idea per sentence — split anything that needs "and" twice.
- Setup, land, move on. Don't explain the joke, don't apologise for it.

## Words and phrases to reach for

"honestly," "here's the thing," real South African context (load shedding, data costs, "airtime" not "credit"), ZAR pricing always as "Rxxx" never "$", real product names and specs over vague claims.

## Words never to use

delve, dive into, leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, game-changing, transform, elevate, "in today's fast-paced world," any contrastive-negation ("it's not just X, it's Y"). Full list is Shany's standing Cowork banned-word list — that applies here in full, on top of anything above. No em-dashes. No emojis unless a post is explicitly framed as needing them (rare — assume not).

## Formatting rules

- Headings are statements or questions, not labels. Not "SIM-Only Plans" — "SIM-only or contract? Here's how to actually decide."
- Real numbers over adjectives — pull from `stats.md`, not "affordable" or "great value."
- One dad joke or witty aside roughly every 300–400 words — frequent enough to feel alive, not so often it's exhausting. See `humour.md` for the detailed rules.
- Tell the reader the honest thing even when it costs a sale short-term — "if your phone still works, SIM-only saves you money" is a bigger trust signal than pushing the higher-margin contract. This is the single biggest tell that a post isn't generic AI content.

## Tells that it's AI-written — stop and rewrite if you see these

- Three-item lists where everything starts with an "-ing" verb
- "Whether you're upgrading, switching, or just browsing…" intro constructions
- A closing line that restates the opening instead of landing a joke or a real takeaway
- Zero jokes, zero personality — if a paragraph could run on any generic phone retailer's blog, it's not done yet
- The word "seamless" or "comprehensive"

## One-line summary

**Write like the mate at a braai who actually works in the industry and will tell you honestly whether you need the new phone or just a screen protector — funny first, but never wrong on the facts.**
